In the GEANT4 example B4a, the simulated detector is a sampling calorimeter. It consists of a rectangular box made of many identical layers stacked along the beam direction. 
Each layer contains an absorber plate of lead followed by a sensitive gap that houses liquid argon, where energy deposition is recorded. A 10 GeV incident particle, either e-, 
proton or alpha particle is simulated traversing the calorimeter, producing showers through successive interactions with matter, we study the energy deposition of different 
particles and how changing the absorber material affects the simulation. (I have also used the run1 macro to produce a small root file for each simulation)
